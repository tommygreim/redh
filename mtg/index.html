Strixhaven Tabletop

A minimal, rules-assisted Magic: The Gathering client.

Both players are controlled by you (hotseat). The app handles turns, phases, mana, the stack, and combat math. Card text that isn't automated is resolved manually with the per-card action buttons and the board tools — so every card in the set is playable.

Tip: tap lands for mana, then Cast. Use ＋Card to drop any card straight onto the board.
